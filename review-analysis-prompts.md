# Customer Review Analysis Prompts

## Prompt 1 – Review Sentiment Analysis

Act as an ecommerce product analyst.

Analyze the following customer reviews and identify overall sentiment.

Reviews:
1. "The fabric quality is excellent and very comfortable."
2. "The color faded after one wash."
3. "Perfect for daily wear, very breathable cotton."

Return:

• Overall sentiment (Positive / Neutral / Negative)  
• Main positive themes  
• Main negative themes  
• Suggestions for product improvement

## Prompt 2 – Product Improvement Insights

Act as a marketplace product improvement specialist.

Analyze these customer reviews for a women's cotton kurti set.

Reviews:
1. "Beautiful design but size runs slightly small."
2. "Material is soft but stitching could be stronger."
3. "Very comfortable for summer."

Provide:

• Key strengths customers mention  
• Common complaints  
• Actionable improvements for the product
